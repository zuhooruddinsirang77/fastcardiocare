Know what’s included

What a complete treatment estimate should identify

Use this list to check any written quote. If something is missing, ask the provider before you decide.

  • Provider and facilityNamed, with location
  • Procedure and case assumptionsWhat the plan is based on
  • Surgeon and clinician feesIncluding the anaesthesia team
  • Hospital stayExpected room and ICU days
  • DevicesBrand, type and count where applicable
  • Tests, medicines and suppliesRoutine items included
  • ExclusionsWhat is not covered
  • If care changesLikely charges for changes or a longer stay
  • Quote expiry and currencyHow long the estimate is valid
  • Payment termsDeposit, payment and cancellation
  • Follow-up and recordsPlan for after discharge

The practical details

Travel and support costs are shown separately

So you can see the cost of medical care clearly, practical costs are listed on their own and never mixed into the treatment estimate.

How FastCardioCare is paid

We will explain any coordination fee, referral arrangement or commercial relationship with a provider in plain language before you commit to anything.

Fee model and provider arrangements: to be confirmed and reviewed by counsel

  • FlightsPatient and companion
  • Visa or invitation supportIncluding government fees
  • TransportAirport and local transfers
  • HotelBefore or after admission
  • Meals and companion costsDay-to-day expenses
  • InterpretationWhere needed
  • Coordination servicesShown as its own line
  • Optional activitiesOnly if you ask for them

Important price note

An estimate is not a guarantee of the final bill. Clinical findings, complications, extra devices or a longer stay may change charges. The provider should explain the process before elective care proceeds, subject to emergency clinical needs.
