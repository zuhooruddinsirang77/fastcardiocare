Travel & support costs

Clear costs.
A better-planned journey.

Travel and support costs are shown separately from your medical estimate, so you can see what each part of your journey includes.

  • Flights & visa assistanceTravel requirements for your destination.

    Ask about flight flexibility, visa requirements, invitation letters and any application fees. Travel bookings should follow confirmation of your care plan and appointment dates.

  • Airport & local transportTransfers and practical travel arrangements.

    Confirm arrival times, transfer locations, accessibility needs and whether patient and companion transport are both included in the quote.

  • Patient & companion stayAccommodation around your appointments.

    Confirm the accommodation type, distance from the hospital, number of nights, companion occupancy and cancellation terms.

  • Coordinator supportAgree the support and any fees in advance.

    Agree the appointment, communication and records support you need. Ask for the scope, availability and coordination charges in writing.

Important price note

An estimate is not a guarantee of the final bill. Clinical findings, complications, extra devices or a longer stay may change charges. Ask the provider to explain potential changes and confirm any coordination fees or commercial arrangements before you commit.

Before you decide

Your estimate questions, answered.

Explore the practical details before requesting a written quotation.

01Can I get a fixed price from this page?

Pricing is case-specific. The treating provider needs to review your records and proposed care before issuing a quotation. The treatment cards show points to confirm, rather than guaranteed inclusions or published rates.

02Are travel and accommodation included?

Travel, accommodation and agreed coordination costs are listed separately from medical care. Ask for a written breakdown for your patient and companion arrangements.

03What should I prepare for an enquiry?

Start with your contact details, preferred destination and a brief description of the support you need. The team can explain the next steps and how records are shared with your permission. Please avoid sending medical details in the initial contact form.

04Can the final bill change?

Yes. Clinical findings, additional tests or devices, complications and a longer stay can affect the bill. Ask the treating provider to explain exclusions, possible extra charges and the process for changes.
