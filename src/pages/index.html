Your heart-care journey

Focused on the heart. Here for the whole journey.

Heart treatment can involve difficult decisions, multiple providers and travel planning. Our team focuses on cardiac care and helps organise the nonclinical details around your provider’s care plan.

Your treating clinicians make all medical decisions.

A journey as personal as you.Clarity and support, one step at a time.

Step 1 of 7Your starting point

Someone you love needs heart care. Start here.

A recommended heart procedure can bring questions about treatment, providers, costs and travel. We help you understand the process and prepare for an independent provider review, one step at a time.

How the care journey works

No commitment to treatment or travel.

Step 2 of 7You start with a conversation

Share a few details, in confidence.

Tell us how to reach you and which heart-care question you are exploring. A coordinator explains how the process works, how privacy and consent are handled, and what happens next.

No medical records are needed at this stage, and an enquiry does not commit you to treatment or travel.

  • Records shared only with your consent
  • Clinical decisions: your provider

Step 3 of 7An independent provider reviews your case

Clinicians decide what is clinically appropriate.

With your permission, relevant records go to an independent provider. The treating clinician decides whether more information is needed, whether your case can be assessed, and what options may be appropriate.

FastCardioCare does not diagnose or treat. The provider assesses you, recommends treatment and obtains your clinical consent.

Step 4 of 7You see the plan, and what it includes

A written estimate, explained line by line.

If a provider can offer care, we help you understand the proposed plan and its estimate: the expected stay, included devices and services, exclusions, likely additional charges and follow-up arrangements.

An estimate is not a guarantee of the final bill. Clinical findings, extra devices or a longer stay can change charges.

Step 5 of 7The decision is yours

Take the time you need.

There is no commitment. You can ask questions, seek another opinion, talk it through with your family and your local doctor, and then decide whether to proceed.

Travel is booked only after the provider confirms suitability, an appointment and the practical requirements.

  • Appointments
  • Travel and accommodation
  • Companion arrangements

Step 6 of 7We look after the practical details

Appointments, travel and a place to stay.

If you proceed, we help organise agreed nonclinical items: appointments, travel, accommodation and arrangements for an accompanying family member, subject to provider rules.

The provider remains responsible for your clinical care throughout.

Step 7 of 7Home, with everything you need

Your records travel home with you.

Before you leave, ask the treating provider for the documents your local doctor will need to continue your care, and arrange follow-up with your home clinician where possible.
