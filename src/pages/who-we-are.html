FastCardioCare is an international patient coordination company focused on cardiac care. We help patients and families communicate with independent healthcare providers and organise practical parts of a treatment journey.

The care provider assesses the patient, recommends treatment, obtains clinical consent and delivers care. FastCardioCare does not provide medical care or replace a patient’s local doctor.

Clear roles, from the first conversation

Knowing who is responsible for what helps you get the right care, avoid confusion,
and feel confident at every stage.

FastCardioCare coordinates

  • Explains the process, privacy and consent
  • Shares records with a provider only with your permission
  • Helps clarify written estimates and what they include
  • Organises agreed nonclinical items: appointments, travel, accommodation, companion logistics
  • Helps with discharge paperwork and records handover

Your care provider treats

  • Reviews your records and decides whether it can assess you
  • Diagnoses and recommends treatment
  • Obtains your clinical consent
  • Delivers and is responsible for medical care
  • Provides discharge instructions and a follow-up plan

You decide

  • Whether to request a provider review
  • Whether to accept a proposed plan and estimate
  • Whether to seek another opinion
  • Whether and when to travel
  • How to involve your local doctor before and after care
