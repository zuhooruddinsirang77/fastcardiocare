Who we are

FastCardioCare (legal company name, state of formation and address: to be confirmed) provides patient coordination and travel-related support. We are not a healthcare provider.

What we collect through this website

  • The contact details you give us: name, email, phone, country of residence, preferred contact method, time and language.
  • Whether you are the patient, a family member or a referral partner, and an optional broad area of interest.
  • Any short message you choose to send.
  • Basic technical information needed to run and secure the website.

We ask you not to send diagnoses, test results, passport scans or medical records through the website form.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to your enquiry and explain our coordination process. We do not send your message text or areas of interest to advertising platforms.

Sharing health information with providers

If you want a provider review, we will ask for your specific permission first. We will explain who receives your records, why, and how they are protected. We share only what is needed.

How we protect it

Your enquiry is sent over an encrypted connection and stored in an access-controlled system. Only authorised staff can see it. Storage location, vendors and retention period: to be confirmed
