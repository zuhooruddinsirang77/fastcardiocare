Our five principles

01

Cardiac focus

Our service is designed around heart-care journeys, not a broad catalogue of unrelated treatments.

02

Provider options

Depending on the case and current availability, more than one provider or destination may be considered. A provider must first decide whether it can assess and treat the patient.

03

Clear estimates

We ask for written estimates that identify the proposed care, expected hospital stay, device assumptions, exclusions and possible extra charges.

04

Family-aware coordination

We can help plan practical arrangements for an accompanying family member, subject to provider rules and the patient’s needs.

05

Respect for clinical independence

Providers make diagnosis and treatment decisions. Patients can ask questions, seek another opinion and decide whether to proceed.

Honest about what we can and cannot do

Trust starts with clear limits. Here is what to expect from us.

What you can expect

  • The provider and facility named before you decide
  • Estimates explained in plain language, with exclusions
  • Your permission requested before records are shared
  • Any fee or commercial relationship explained at the right point
  • Time to involve your family and local doctor

What we will not do

  • Diagnose, treat or give medical advice
  • Promise outcomes, eligibility or shorter waits
  • Promise savings or a fixed final bill
  • Decide your destination before a provider has reviewed your case
  • Pressure you to travel or accept a proposal
