How it works

A clear, step-by-step
care journey

From your first enquiry to discharge and records handover, you will know what happens next, who is responsible and where the decisions are yours.

Start a Conversation See the Seven Steps

An enquiry carries no commitment to treatment or travel.

The seven steps

  1. 1

    1. Tell us what support you need

    Send a short enquiry. Do not send emergency requests through the website form.

    You
  2. 2

    2. We explain the coordination process

    A coordinator contacts you, explains privacy and consent, and tells you how records can be shared securely if you want a provider review.

    FastCardioCare
  3. 3

    3. An independent provider reviews your case

    With your authorisation, relevant records go to the provider. The treating clinician decides whether more information is needed, whether the case can be assessed, and what options may be appropriate.

    Your authorisation firstProvider
  4. 4

    4. Review the proposed care and estimate

    If a provider offers a plan, we help clarify the estimate, expected stay, included devices and services, exclusions, likely additional charges and follow-up arrangements.

    ProviderFastCardioCare
  5. 5

    5. Decide whether to proceed

    You decide whether to accept a provider’s proposal. Travel should be booked only after the provider confirms suitability, appointment and practical requirements.

    Decision point: yours
  6. 6

    6. Coordinate the visit

    If you proceed, we help organise agreed nonclinical items such as appointments, travel, accommodation and companion logistics. The provider remains responsible for clinical care.

    FastCardioCare: logisticsProvider: clinical care
  7. 7

    7. Plan discharge and follow-up

    Before departure, request discharge instructions, medication list, procedure report, images where available and a follow-up plan for your home clinician.
