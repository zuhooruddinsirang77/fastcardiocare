Let’s Talk

Need urgent care?

This website is not monitored for emergencies. Contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest emergency department now.

Where will I receive treatment?

The destination depends on your clinical needs, provider availability, budget and preferences. We will explain the provider and facility before you decide. We do not promise a particular destination before a provider has reviewed the case.

Does FastCardioCare provide medical treatment?

No. FastCardioCare coordinates communication and agreed travel-related support. Independent licensed providers assess patients and deliver medical care.

Does asking for an estimate mean I have to travel?

No. An enquiry or estimate request does not commit you to treatment or travel.

Will I receive a fixed price?

A provider may give an estimate based on the information available. The final charges can change if the clinical plan changes, extra devices or tests are needed, or the stay is extended. Ask the provider to explain exclusions and additional-charge rules. See what a complete estimate should include.

Can you guarantee a particular result or shorter wait?

No. We cannot guarantee clinical outcomes, eligibility, appointment timing or shorter waits. These depend on the individual case and the independent provider.

Can my family travel with me?

Often a companion can travel, but hospital access, accommodation and visa rules vary. We can help clarify practical arrangements for the selected provider and destination.

How do you handle my medical records?

We ask for your permission before sharing records with a provider. Before you share anything, a coordinator will explain who receives your records, why, how they are protected and how to ask questions.

Please do not upload or paste full medical records into the general enquiry form. If you want a provider review, we will give you a secure way to share records after you give consent. Read our Privacy Notice.

What if I need urgent care?

Do not wait for a response from FastCardioCare. Contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest emergency department.

What follow-up will I need at home?

Ask the treating provider for a written discharge plan and, where possible, arrange follow-up with your local clinician before you travel. The exact plan depends on your care.
