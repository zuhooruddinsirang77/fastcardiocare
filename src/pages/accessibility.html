Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • We design every page and form to be usable with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Pages use clear headings, landmarks and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Form fields have labels, hints and error messages that screen readers can announce.
  • Text and controls are designed to meet colour-contrast requirements.
  • Layouts adapt to phones, tablets and desktops, and support browser zoom.
  • Motion is reduced when your device asks for reduced motion.

Tell us about a problem

If you find something hard to use, please tell us at accessibility contact: to be confirmed. We will try to provide the information in another way and fix the issue.
